Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Overview

K3B Sports Management, LLC ("K3B," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of every athlete and visitor who interacts with our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit k3bsports.com or complete our athlete assessment.

By using our website or submitting an assessment, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

When you complete the K3B athlete assessment or create an account, we collect the following information:

  • Full name and email address
  • Sport and competitive level
  • Assessment responses across our five evaluation areas
  • Account login credentials (passwords are stored securely)
  • Any notes or communications you share with the K3B team

We may also collect standard website analytics data such as page views, browser type, and general location to improve our platform.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to deliver K3B's services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Evaluate your assessment responses and generate your athlete profile
  • Communicate with you about your application status and consultation
  • Build and deliver a custom engagement plan if you are accepted
  • Maintain your athlete portal and account history
  • Improve the quality and accuracy of our evaluation process

Confidentiality

K3B takes confidentiality seriously. All assessment findings and recommendations are private and shared only with the athlete and any representatives the athlete explicitly approves. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

K3B complies with the highest standards of professional and medical ethics in handling sensitive athlete information.

Data Storage

Your assessment data and account information are stored securely. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at info@k3bsports.com.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and remember your session. We do not use cookies to track you across third-party websites or serve targeted advertising. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Questions

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at info@k3bsports.com or visit our contact page.
