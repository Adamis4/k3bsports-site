K3B Sports Management was built on a simple belief: elite athletes deserve more than contract negotiations and highlight reels. They deserve a team that understands the full complexity of their lives and is equipped to support every dimension of it.

Athletes today face unprecedented pressure. The demands of performance, media scrutiny, financial complexity, and the inevitable transition out of sport create challenges that most management companies are simply not built to address.

K3B is different. Our 360-degree evaluation model brings together a trusted network of domain experts spanning mental health, physical wellness, legal and financial advisory, public image strategy, and lifestyle planning. We do not offer one-size-fits-all packages. Every engagement is custom-built around the individual athlete.

We are selective by design. We take on athletes we can truly impact, and when we do, we go all in.

Our Mission
Helping athletes perform at their best on and off the field.

The 360-degree evaluation identifies unseen risks and opportunities that most athletes and their teams miss entirely. Financial exposure before it becomes a crisis. Mental health challenges before they impact performance. Brand vulnerabilities before they become headlines.

Beyond identifying problems, K3B is built to solve them. Our network of domain experts does not just evaluate, they advise, support, and stay engaged throughout the athlete's career and beyond.
